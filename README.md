## About

<img align="right" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mishakorzik/mishakorzik/main/gitline.gif"/>

Final-year **Software Engineering** student working at the intersection of **NLP research** and **production ML systems**.

My undergraduate thesis focuses on **Bangla Fake News Detection** — fine-tuning BanglaBERT, mBERT, and XLM-RoBERTa to tackle misinformation in low-resource Indic languages.

Outside the thesis, I build end-to-end ML pipelines: from dataset curation and model training to containerized deployment with FastAPI and Docker.

**Currently exploring:** MLOps, model compression, and cross-lingual transfer learning.
